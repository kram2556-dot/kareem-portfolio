# Luxury Portfolio Direction

## Chosen direction: Quiet Luxury / Architectural Editorial

- **Design movement:** contemporary quiet luxury with editorial architecture cues.
- **Core principles:** generous whitespace, cinematic photography, warm metallic accents, and confident typography.
- **Color philosophy:** champagne gold `#c5a059` as a signature detail; warm ivory and stone for calm; midnight navy `#0d1117` for authority.
- **Layout paradigm:** asymmetric editorial composition with offset image frames, horizontal project index, and short high-impact copy blocks.
- **Signature elements:** hairline gold rules, corner labels, and a floating WhatsApp concierge action.
- **Interaction philosophy:** calm, tactile micro-interactions; every action leads to a clear consultation path.
- **Animation:** restrained reveal-on-scroll, gentle image zoom on project cards, and a soft WhatsApp pulse.
- **Typography:** Cairo for Arabic and DM Sans for English/UI; display headings are large, light, and tightly tracked.
- **Brand essence:** م. كريم الشريف يقدم قرارات تصميمية مدروسة تحول المساحات الراقية إلى تجارب شخصية قابلة للعيش.
- **Voice:** assured, precise, and warm. Example lines: «نصمم الهدوء الذي تراه» / “Spaces with a point of view.”
- **Wordmark:** compact bilingual lockup with an architectural crosshair mark and a gold baseline.
- **Signature brand color:** warm champagne gold `#c5a059`.

## Asset direction

Selected Unsplash interior imagery is used as local project photography for the hero and portfolio cards. Images are treated with a dark cinematic overlay so the gold details remain legible.
